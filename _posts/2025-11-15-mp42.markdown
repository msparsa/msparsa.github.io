We trained MP4, a transformer-based model that maps natural language prompts to protein sequences, on a dataset of 3.2 billion points and 138k tokens. In a benchmark of 96 prompts representing a wide array of functions and contexts, MP4 excelled by simultaneously improving on three key metrics: sequence realism, predicted fold quality, and alignment to the requested function.
