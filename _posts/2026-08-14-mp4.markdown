MP4 is a transformer that maps plain-language specifications directly to protein sequences, sidestepping the dependence on known folds and homologous templates that constrains conventional design. Across a 96-prompt benchmark it improves sequence realism, predicted fold quality, and functional alignment simultaneously from text input alone, and two designs were experimentally expressed, crystallized at 1.30 Å and 1.77 Å — one revealing a novel fold — with ATP binding and hydrolysis confirmed in vitro.
